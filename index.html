The Slack & Asymmetry

Industrial safety compliance in the UAE relies on text-heavy documentation administered in English or Arabic. For the significant demographic of chemical handlers originating from South Asia with limited literacy in formal administrative scripts, mandatory onboarding and daily clinic screening represent major cognitive bottlenecks. Plant operators face persistent worker turnover and audit friction during Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE) inspections, despite workers possessing high operational competence in handling hazardous substances.

The market asymmetry lies in the separation between rigid enterprise EHS software suites and the simple reality of frontline check-in desks. By replacing text-based forms with icon-driven UI and voice-native prompts in Hindi, Urdu, and Malayalam, facilities can capture accurate screening data instantly without requiring literacy [est: saves 18 minutes per handler check-in, reducing queue times at JAFZA Gate 4 clinics by over 40%].

The Wedge & 90-Day MVP

We are bypassing enterprise procurement cycles by deploying a standalone, mobile-first Progressive Web Application (PWA) onto rugged tablets positioned directly at a single clinic check-in desk within a JAFZA hazardous storage facility. The system interfaces cleanly with existing registry workflows, avoiding costly rip-and-replace integrations with legacy plant databases.

Pricing Intent & Economics

Pricing is structured around facility checkpoints rather than per-seat licenses, aligning with the variable influx of contract labor. A flat quarterly retainer per clinic desk covers PWA maintenance, audio prompt localization, and registry synchronization. This sidesteps complex enterprise software contract negotiations and positions the product as an operational utility line item rather than an IT capital expenditure.
